Tembak Makna

Shoot the Meaning — a Bahasa Melayu vocabulary game

How to play
1

Read the phrase

A Malay phrase appears at the top of the screen and is read aloud. Press L to hear it again.

2

Find the right room

Each doorway leads to a room for one category. Work out which category the phrase belongs to and walk into that room.

3

Fight past the drones

Guard drones patrol the hub in front of the doors and shoot back. Clear them or dodge past — they never follow you into a room.

4

Shoot the meaning

Answer panels hang on the walls inside. You must be standing in the room to answer — no shooting through the doorway.

5

Spend at the armoury

The lit console at the side of the hub sells better weapons. Walk up, press E. What you spend comes off your final score.

WASD or arrow keys — walk  ·  Shift — run  ·  Mouse — look  ·  Click — shoot  ·  L — replay audio  ·  Esc — pause
